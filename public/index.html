Your connection, explained.

  1. Camera → your server. At home, put them on the same reachable Wi-Fi/LAN. On a VPS, join the VPS and cameras to the same private network, then use the camera’s private hostname or address.
  2. Your browser → your server. For remote access, join that private network too and use the server’s private HTTPS address. Tailscale Serve is one option; other VPNs work if they provide the same private routing.
  3. Keep both paths private. Use a strong server password, restrict VPN access to your devices and keep the app port closed to the public internet. Do not forward camera ports or turn on a public tunnel.

This server relays live video. Its administrator can access saved viewing links and camera feeds. Use a machine you trust; protect its data directory and backups.

CamWeave Camera must stay open. Locking the iPhone or backgrounding the camera pauses capture. This version has no recording or alerts.
